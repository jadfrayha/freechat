The rules

  1. You must be 18 or older.
  2. Be decent. No harassment, hate, threats, or bullying.
  3. No sexual content, and never anything involving minors. We report it to the authorities.
  4. No spam, ads, scams, or links. Links are removed automatically.
  5. Protect yourself: don't share your name, number, address, or socials with strangers.
  6. If someone makes you uncomfortable, press Skip. You won't be matched with them again.

Privacy

Messages go straight from you to the other person. We don't save them.

We don't ask for your name, email, or phone number.

To limit spam, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address only while you're connected.

No ads and no tracking cookies.
